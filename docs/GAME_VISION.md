# Game Vision

Cozy Pixel Chess is a cozy multiplayer chess game built around themed pixel-art rooms, soft social play, and beginner-friendly learning. It should feel less like a competitive chess site and more like sitting at a little table in a charming room while rain, snow, candles, or starlight set the mood.

The long-term game includes online private rooms, local play, teaching and puzzle modes, collectible themes, avatars, decorative room items, board frames, piece sets, ambient sounds, small animations, and gentle post-game feedback.

The game is not trying to replace Chess.com or Lichess for hardcore players. It should serve cozy game fans, beginners, casual chess players, and players who want a cute social chess space without harsh feedback or pressure.

Core pillars:

- Cozy multiplayer chess with private rooms first.
- Pixel-art themed rooms that can be collected and customized.
- Teaching and puzzle content that encourages learning without shame.
- Cosmetic progression through themes, avatars, frames, decor, sounds, and particles.
- Relaxing atmosphere with soft motion, ambient audio, and kind language.
