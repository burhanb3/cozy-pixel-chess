# MVP Scope

The first realistic MVP is a focused Rainy Cafe local chess experience.

## In Scope

- One theme: Rainy Cafe.
- Offline playable chess using `chess.js`.
- Basic local play on one device.
- Legal move validation.
- Move history.
- Simple player cards.
- Theme-aware UI shell.
- Placeholder avatar and decor slots.
- A modular asset structure for future PNG/WebP/SVG/game assets.
- Documentation that guides future Codex sessions.

## Later In The MVP Track

- Five beginner puzzles.
- Private room online multiplayer.
- Small cosmetic preview.
- Better piece and board assets.
- Gentle end-game summary.

## Explicitly Out Of Scope For The First MVP

- Public matchmaking.
- Accounts and auth.
- Real-money purchases.
- Full shop economy.
- Full puzzle campaign.
- Advanced engine analysis.
- Anti-cheat systems.
- Timers and rating systems.
- Multiple finished art themes.
