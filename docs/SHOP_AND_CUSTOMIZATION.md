# Shop And Customization

Customization should feel like collecting cozy room pieces, not aggressive monetization.

## Planned Cosmetic Types

- Unlockable themes.
- Avatars.
- Avatar frames.
- Table decor.
- Window decorations.
- Board frames.
- Chess piece sets.
- Tablecloths.
- Piece boxes.
- Particle effects.
- Ambient sound packs.

## Economy Direction

- Use in-game soft currency later.
- No real-money monetization in the MVP.
- Keep unlocks cosmetic and non-competitive.
- Avoid pressure, fear-of-missing-out, or punitive reward language.

Shop and customization systems are intentionally not implemented in the foundation task.
